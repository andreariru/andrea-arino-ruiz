ABOUT ME

Andrea Ariño Ruiz

3D Character Animator

Combining analytical thinking with creativity to bring characters and stories to life.

I'm a 3D Character Animator with over 5 years of professional experience across TV series, feature films, short films, documentaries and gameplay animation.

Throughout my career, I've had the opportunity to work with international teams and adapt to different production pipelines, workflows and animation styles. I particularly enjoy character acting, dialogue shots and bringing subtle performances to life through body mechanics and facial animation.

With a background in engineering and product development, I bring an analytical and structured approach to creative work. I'm a fast learner who values collaboration, continuous learning and delivering high-quality animation while meeting production deadlines.
